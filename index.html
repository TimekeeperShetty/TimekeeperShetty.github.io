Spatial AudioDCASE 2024

Sound Event Localization & Detection

Which 3D-audio architecture actually holds up when sources overlap?

Reproduced two DCASE 2024 SELD baselines end-to-end on STARSS23 - a multi-channel ambisonic pipeline with Log-Mel + intensity-vector features, comparing CRNN + self-attention (SELDnet) against track-wise permutation-invariant training (EINV2), with joint SED + DOA regression heads and ADPIT loss.

Track-wise PIT wins on polyphonic, overlapping-source scenes. Applies to head-tracked binaural rendering - Vision Pro, Quest, AirPods.

Generative DSPReal-time

Spectral Phase Vocoder

An STFT engine with three creative modes, built to survive a real-time plugin's constraints.

Per-bin instantaneous-frequency estimation with phase-wrap correction keeps phase coherent across overlap-add frames; three parallel modes - channel vocoder, cross-synthesis, and spectral freeze - share Mel-band envelopes for formant-aware shaping, with auto-normalized mix weights so blending never drifts loudness.

Maps directly to VST/AU parameter automation. Currently porting the DSP core to JUCE for real-time.

Machine ListeningTeam of 4

Multi-Encoder Automatic Audio Captioning

Matching giant ensembles at a fraction of the size.

A DCASE 2024 captioning system: frozen BEATs + ConvNeXt + AST encoders feeding a Conformer post-encoder and BART decoder, with three fusion strategies ablated. Inference adds nucleus sampling, CLAP audio-text reranking, and LLM post-processing.

FENSE 0.531 at 355M params - 95% smaller than the 6B-param top ensemble, at competitive metrics.

Audio MLDomain transfer

Instrument Classification: Synthetic → Real

How far does a model trained on synthetic stems get on real, messy recordings?

A Slakh -> OpenMIC transfer pipeline with a frozen CLAP waveform encoder and MLP head, custom stem-remix augmentation, class-balanced sampling, and per-class thresholding — benchmarked against log-Mel CNN baselines with full W&B tracking.

Isolated domain mismatch, not data volume, as the real bottleneck — measured on-pipeline rather than assumed.

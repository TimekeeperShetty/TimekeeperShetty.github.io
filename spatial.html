Spatial AudioDCASE 2024

Sound Event Localization & Detection

Which 3D-audio design choices actually hold up when sources overlap?

Reproduced the DCASE 2024 Task 3 audio-only baseline end-to-end on STARSS23 across five configurations, each adjacent pair differing in exactly one variable: microphone format (first-order Ambisonics vs. tetrahedral array), spatial features (Mel + intensity vectors, GCC-PHAT, SALSA-Lite), and output representation (Single- vs. Multi-ACCDOA with ADPIT permutation-invariant loss), on a ~0.5M-parameter CRNN.

Best config (FOA + Mel/IV + Multi-ACCDOA): F20 7.4%, 38.2° DOA error, 0.788 SELD score - reproducing the published baseline's task ordering and landing within 1.3° of its DOA on one-fifth the training budget. Output representation dominated everything else: F20 7.4% vs. 0.3% for Multi- vs. Single-ACCDOA, format and features held constant.

Generative DSPSTFT · from scratch

Spectral Phase Vocoder

An STFT engine that runs three creative modes in parallel and blends them in the complex domain.

Built from scratch on numpy.fft - no DSP library doing the vocoding. Per-bin instantaneous-frequency estimation with phase-wrap correction keeps phase coherent across overlap-add frames, which is what stops the spectral freeze from degenerating into a static buzz. Three parallel modes - channel vocoder, cross-synthesis, and spectral freeze - share Mel-band envelopes for formant-aware shaping, summed as complex spectra with auto-normalized mix weights so blending changes character, not loudness.

2048-point STFT at a 512-sample hop (4× overlap, 48 kHz), 24 Mel bands for the formant envelope, and three modes on a continuous weight simplex rather than a mode switch. Seven rendered blends and the spectrogram analysis ship with the repo.

Machine ListeningTeam of 4

Multi-Encoder Automatic Audio Captioning

Matching giant ensembles at a fraction of the size.

A DCASE 2024 captioning system on Clotho v2: frozen BEATs + ConvNeXt + AST encoders feeding a Conformer post-encoder and BART decoder, with three fusion strategies ablated and AudioCaps + Clotho mixup pretraining. Inference adds nucleus sampling, CLAP audio-text reranking, and LLM post-processing.

Sequence fusion led the ablation at FENSE 0.531 on 355M parameters - 95% smaller than the 6B-parameter top ensemble, at competitive metrics. Pretraining, not encoder count, was what fixed the parameter bloat on a 6,972-clip dataset.

Audio MLDomain transfer

Instrument Classification: Synthetic → Real

How far does a model trained on synthetic stems get on real, messy recordings?

A Slakh → OpenMIC transfer pipeline benchmarking three encoders - a from-scratch log-Mel CNN, frozen CLAP, and AudioMAE - each with an MLP head, plus stem-remix and pedalboard reverb/gain/noise augmentation, class-balanced sampling, and per-class thresholding, with full W&B tracking.

The log-Mel CNN won in-domain (0.92 micro-F1) then collapsed on real audio (0.40). CLAP traded in-domain accuracy for robustness and held 0.60 synth→real, cutting the transfer drop from 52 to 29 points. Augmentation alone bought nothing on real data (0.58 vs. 0.60) - isolating domain mismatch, not data volume, as the bottleneck.

Embedded AudioESP32 · C/C++

MEAP-Markov: Generative Raag on an ESP32

A Markov chain improvising in raag Hamir, synthesized entirely on the microcontroller.

A first-order Markov chain over a nine-pitch Hamir set, with transition tallies normalized into a row-wise CDF at boot so note selection costs one uniform draw and a short walk - cheap enough to run inside a 128 Hz control callback. Synthesis is on-device via Mozzi: three triangle oscillators harmonized by stepping through the raag's own note set, a 3-operator FM voice, plate reverb, a resonant lowpass, and flash-resident sample playback, mixed at audio rate with live pot, touch-pad, and DIP control. No host computer in the audio path.

Real-time synthesis on an ESP32-S3 under hard constraints - control-rate and audio-rate work kept strictly separate, int8 wavetables, samples compiled into flash with no filesystem.
